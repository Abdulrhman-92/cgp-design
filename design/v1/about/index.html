Born From
Obsession.

CGP began the way every obsession does — one builder, one workbench, and a refusal to accept “good enough”. What started as hand-bent loops for friends became Riyadh’s bespoke forge for clients who wanted machines no catalogue could offer.

We kept the atelier discipline: no assembly line, no BOM shortcuts, no “close enough” routing. Every commission is engineered as a one-off — its own loop design, its own material palette, its own signature.

That is why our clients don’t buy a spec sheet. They commission a machine the way you commission a suit — measured, fitted, and signed off by the hands that built it.

// SYS_PROCESS

The Forging
Process.

Four stages stand between your brief and the machine that arrives at your door. Nothing ships until it survives all four.

  1. 01

    The Blueprint

    Every commission starts as a private consultation. Hardware, loop routing, and materials are mapped to the millimeter before a single tool is lifted.

  2. 02

    The Sourcing

    Only top-binned silicon and aerospace-grade fittings make the shortlist. Every component is verified, serialized, and logged before it enters the forge.

  3. 03

    The Forge

    Tubes are bent by hand, loops are routed like circuitry, and cable runs are dressed until the interior reads like architecture.

  4. 04

    The Baptism

    48 hours of pressure and thermal stress testing. The machine does not leave the forge until it proves it can survive yours.
